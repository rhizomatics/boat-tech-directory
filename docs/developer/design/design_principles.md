# Design Principles

The principles boat-tech-directory is designed by. They start from the [Rhizomatics HQ policies](https://hq.rhizomatics.org.uk/policies/) that every
product follows. Sections specific to boat-tech-directory are added here as its design settles, usually through an RFC.

## Scope

A curated [Awesome list](https://awesome.re) of boat tech: NMEA and Seatalk, marine internet, open source projects,
hardware vendors, standards, education, blogs, vlogs and forums, with a focus on open source and standards. It's
published as a site and a SignalK webapp from the same list.

## Editorial principles

- Best endeavours, by sailors for sailors: not exhaustive or authoritative, and no recommendation of any vendor
  is implied.
- No advertisements, paid placements or referral links.
- Openly licensed ([CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)), so anyone can reuse it.
- Follows the Awesome list format, checked by `awesome-lint`.

## How the design changes

- Major changes are planned as RFCs in `docs/developer/design/rfcs/` ([design policy](https://hq.rhizomatics.org.uk/policies/design/)). They're
  listed with every other Rhizomatics product's on the [HQ RFCs page](https://hq.rhizomatics.org.uk/rfcs/).
- Expected work, large and small, goes in a `roadmap.md` next to this file.
- Releases of the site and webapp follow the [release policy](https://hq.rhizomatics.org.uk/policies/release/): semantic versions, immutable, built and published by
  GitHub Actions.
- Everything else follows the [project standards](https://hq.rhizomatics.org.uk/policies/project_standards/) shared by all Rhizomatics repos.
